# Engagement expansion — staged, local first
Implemented in source: 100 stages, six named worlds, rival names, boss labels at world ends, deterministic openings for stages 31–100, new derived achievements, tie-break AI styles above 30, capture-chain feedback, and post-match descriptive metrics. Historical stages 1–30 retain existing board sizes, AI difficulty and saved opening rules. A single edge can claim at most TWO cells; triple/quad are cumulative on a single uninterrupted turn, not a single move. No cognitive ability claims.

Not yet complete: authored and playtested campaign puzzles, meaningful distinct boss behavior, unlockable rival avatar rewards, Infinity, persistent performance history and strategic review, 50 achievements, separate Blitz/Survival/Puzzle Master modes, weekly missions, season pass, all seasonal home screen runtime designs, entitlement verification. Online matches, private room codes, ranked leaderboards and tournaments need authenticated authoritative server state, reconnect handling, abuse protection and anti-cheat; no mock online ranks. Existing Daily Challenge is the local puzzle base. Keep paid content cosmetic only. No real purchase enabled. Test npm locally, build Vite, verify Android, no Actions. No production merge before QA.

## Offline Challenge Arena
Four independent practice modes now have source-level implementations: Puzzle Master (capture/safe/chain puzzle rotation), Perfect Move (maximum immediate double capture), Blitz (ten seconds per human decision; timeout loses), Survival (consecutive round wins with increasing board size and AI difficulty; difficulty caps at round five). No extra currency or Career/Daily rewards. Results are slot-scoped, limited to the latest 300 completed sessions. 7/30-day results show actual practice accuracy, decision time, captures, concessions and survival round; not a cognitive assessment. Timing pauses on native/visibility/page transitions. Unfinished Arcade sessions are intentionally transient when leaving the page or relaunching. No online system, new paid entitlements, or APK. Source tests added but must be run locally before merge. Check portrait layout and lifecycle on Android.
