## Offline Challenge Arena
Four independent practice modes now have source-level implementations: Puzzle Master (capture/safe/chain puzzle rotation), Perfect Move (maximum immediate double capture), Blitz (ten seconds per human decision; timeout loses), Survival (consecutive round wins with increasing board size and AI difficulty; difficulty caps at round five). No extra currency or Career/Daily rewards. Results are slot-scoped, limited to the latest 300 completed sessions. 7/30-day results show actual practice accuracy, decision time, captures, concessions and survival round; not a cognitive assessment. Timing pauses on native/visibility/page transitions. Unfinished Arcade sessions are intentionally transient when leaving the page or relaunching. No online system, new paid entitlements, or APK. Source tests added but must be run locally before merge. Check portrait layout and lifecycle on Android.
